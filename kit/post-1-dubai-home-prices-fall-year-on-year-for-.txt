Setting the tone for the week.

Dubai home prices fall year-on-year for first time since 2021.

For UAE corporates positioned across global trade corridors, energy channels, and cross-border funding lines, the strategic implication lands ahead of where headline coverage catches it. Scenario planning that uses last cycle's assumptions underprices the regional transmission. The Gulf sits at the crossover of monetary, energy, and trade policy in ways that compound rather than offset across cycles.

Currency-mix, supplier-mix, and funding-mix all need a second look against this. Refresh scenario assumptions on a weekly cadence, watch the policy reaction rather than the price reaction, and align procurement and treasury on a single working-capital baseline.

Capital flows respond to policy signals before they respond to underlying fundamentals. Reading the signal early is the differential — confirmation bias is the cost.
